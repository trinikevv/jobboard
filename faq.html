Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the internship application process.

Who is eligible to apply?

The program is open to current students in tertiary institutions, recent graduates, and NYSC members who reside in or are willing to relocate to Benin City, Edo State.

Do I need prior job experience?

No. This is a learning opportunity. You should still have basic foundational knowledge in the role you are applying for.

How long does the internship last?

Most internships run for 3 to 6 months, depending on the role and department.

How will I know if I am selected?

Track your application status from your Student Dashboard. If your status changes to Shortlisted, HR will contact you by phone or email.
