Edo State ICT Agency

Building the next generation of digital leaders in Nigeria.

Our Mission

The Edo State ICT Internship Scheme is an official government initiative designed to bridge the gap between academic learning and real-world technology application. Our program places driven students and recent graduates directly into active government digital projects.

What Interns Do

Interns contribute to real work: writing code for citizen portals, designing interfaces, securing state network infrastructure, and analyzing data that drives policy decisions.

Mentorship

Guidance from experienced tech professionals.

Certification

Official certificate upon successful completion.

Stipend

Competitive monthly stipend support.
